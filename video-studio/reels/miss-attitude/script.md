## Notes to make it better
- Hook A is stronger: the nickname is the scroll-stopper, and "we were sure we knew her" sets up the turn. B states the twist too early.
- Pause a full beat after "Except her." and after "I laughed too." Those two silences carry the guilt.
- If it runs long when you record, cut "Pizza, biryani." first; the scene shows it anyway.
