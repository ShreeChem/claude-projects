## Notes to make it better
- **Hook:** A is stronger - the contradiction lands in the first three seconds, and "your name on it" turns him into the viewer's own dad. Use B if you'd rather open on his words, the way your trailer opens on "I'm fine." Both are about 25 words, so the timing doesn't change.
- **Make it yours:** I wrote it for any father, in English, with the viewer as the child. Swap one item on the 1 a.m. list for something real from your own dad - a sister's wedding fund, a scooter EMI, the one shirt he wears to every function - and it will hit harder. If the read runs past 60 s, cut "Same face. No smile." (Scene 2 already shows it).
- **Pause and sound:** Hold a full beat after "It was love..." before "doing the math" - that's the line that stays. For music, "Between the Words" by Ruiqi Zhao on Epidemic Sound (sad, sentimental solo piano, 72 BPM) sits well under a medium-pace read; add a faint ceiling-fan room tone under the 1 a.m. kitchen scenes (Epidemic Sound: "Ambience, Room Tone, Pulsing, Ceiling Fan").
