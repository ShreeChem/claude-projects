# Life, Unsaid | "Haan, padh raha hoon."

**Format:** YouTube Short / Reel, 9:16, 60 sec, Hinglish
**Theme:** Board exam pressure. A student who says "haan" every day while breaking inside, and parents who think everything is fine.
**Arc:** The video opens on "Haan, padh raha hoon." and ends on the first honest answer he has given all year: "Nahi, mumma."
**Spoken words:** about 139 (roughly 2.5 words per second, leaving room for pauses and one full beat of silence)

---

## The Script

### 0:00–0:04 | HOOK
**Visual:** A dark room with only a desk lamp on. The door opens a few inches and Mumma's silhouette shows in the gap. The boy doesn't look up from his book.
**SFX:** A soft knock on a wooden door.

> **MUMMA (O.S.):** "Padh raha hai na, beta?"
> **BETA (without looking up):** "Haan mumma... padh raha hoon."

**On-screen text:** `"Haan, padh raha hoon."`

---

### 0:04–0:14 | THE LIE
**Visual:** Close-up of the open textbook on page 47. A time-lapse shows the wall clock moving from 9:40 to 10:45 while the page never turns. His pen taps on the page and doesn't write anything.
**SFX:** A clock ticking, a little louder than it would really be.

> **VO (Beta, inner voice, close-mic, low):** "Yeh line main din mein das baar bolta hoon. Ek baar bhi poora sach nahi hota."
> "Book khuli hai. Page 47. Ek ghante se... page 47."

**On-screen text:** `Page 47. 1 ghanta.`

---

### 0:14–0:25 | DINNER
**Visual:** The dinner table under warm tungsten light, with the TV murmuring in the background. Papa puts a roti on the boy's plate and squeezes his shoulder with pride. The boy smiles and nods.

> **PAPA (warm, proud, not harsh):** "Bas yeh ek saal, beta. Phir poori life set."
> **VO:** "Main muskura deta hoon. Unki aankhon mein itni umeed hai... apna darr bolne ki jagah nahi bachti."

**On-screen text:** `Unki umeed > mera darr`

---

### 0:25–0:43 | 2:07 AM
**Visual:** The grade turns cold and blue. He lies awake on his back while the ceiling fan spins slowly above him. His phone lights up his face with **"Mock Test Result: 54%"**. He turns the phone face-down and presses his hand on his chest.
**SFX:** Ceiling fan room tone. The music thins out until it is nearly gone.

> **VO:** "Raat ke do baje neend nahi aati. Seene pe jaise koi baitha ho."
> "Ek hi sawaal — number kam aaye... toh kya main bhi kam pad jaunga?"
> "Mumma-Papa ko lagta hai sab theek hai. Kyunki main khaana kha leta hoon. Has leta hoon. *'Haan'* bol deta hoon."

**On-screen text (one line at a time):** `2:07 AM` → `Mock: 54%` → `"Main theek hoon." (nahi hoon)`

---

### 0:43–0:51 | THE QUESTION
**Visual:** Mumma, up for water, sees light under his door. She comes in and sits on the edge of the bed. She doesn't look at the books. She looks at him.

> **MUMMA (soft):** "Padhai chhod... tu theek hai na?"

**Hold 2 full seconds of TOTAL SILENCE.** Cut the music and the fan. His eyes fill.

> **BETA (barely audible):** "...Nahi, mumma."

**Visual:** She pulls him into a hug. Hold on his hand gripping her dupatta.
**On-screen text:** `Pehli baar... "haan" nahi bola.`

---

### 0:51–1:00 | UNSAID
**Visual:** A slow push-in on the empty desk, with the book still open to page 47. Fade to black.
**Music:** The piano comes back with a single soft phrase.

> **VO (calm, direct to camera / narrator):** "Har *'padh raha hoon'* ke peeche... ek ankaha *'darr lag raha hai'* ho sakta hai."
> "Is baar pehle yeh poochiye — *'tu theek hai?'*"

**End card:**
`Life, Unsaid.`
`Marks ek result hain. Tum nahi.`
`Baat karni ho? Tele-MANAS: 14416 (free, 24x7)`

## Voice & Performance Notes
- **The boy's VO** should be quiet and close to the mic, almost confessional, with no "acting sad." Underplaying it hits harder.
- **Papa is NOT the villain.** His line comes from love, and parents need to see themselves in him so they share the video instead of getting defensive. Avoid any "Sharma ji ka beta" style mockery.
- **The 2-second silence before "Nahi, mumma" is the whole video.** Don't fill it with music or a swoosh.
- **"kam pad jaunga"** does double duty: low marks, and "will I fall short as a person." Give it a tiny pause before it lands.

## Faceless Version (if you don't want actors)
You can shoot every beat with hands and objects only: the door gap with a silhouette, the page 47 time-lapse, hands passing a roti, the ceiling fan, the phone face-down, two hands holding at the end. Use the same VO, with Mumma's and Papa's lines as off-screen voices.
