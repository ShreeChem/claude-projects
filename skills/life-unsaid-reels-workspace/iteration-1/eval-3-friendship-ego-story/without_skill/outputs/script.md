# Life, Unsaid — "730 Status Views"

**Format:** YouTube Shorts / Reels (9:16) + WhatsApp Channel
**Length:** ~58 sec voiceover + 2 sec silent end card = 60 sec
**Theme:** Ego, and the words we never say
**Spoken word count:** 139 words (a slow, steady read with real pauses)
**Voice:** First person, talking straight *to* the friend ("you"). That way the last line works on two levels: it's for the friend, and it's for every viewer who has a "you" of their own.

---

## THE SPOKEN SCRIPT (read this into the mic)

> Two years. Zero texts. Seven hundred and thirty status views.
>
> You watch mine. I watch yours. Every single day. Sometimes… first.
>
> College best friends. One stupid fight. I can't even remember why.
>
> For one whole year, we shared the same room. Two beds. Three feet apart. Same ceiling fan… facing opposite walls. Not one word.
>
> It was never the fight. It was ego. Who goes first. Who looks weak. Who needs who more.
>
> We only talk the way ego allows. You post a song… I know it's for me. I post a quote… you know it's for you.
>
> Here's what ego never let me say. I miss you. I'm sorry. I wasn't even right… I just didn't want to go first.
>
> Ego says wait. Time doesn't. If you're watching this… and you know it's you… my chat's still open.

## VOICE DIRECTION
- Talk quietly, like it's 1 a.m. and your roommate is asleep. No drama in the voice. The words carry the weight.
- Leave a full beat after **"Not one word."** and after **"It was ego."** Those two silences are the emotional peak.
- Go a little faster on "Who goes first. Who looks weak. Who needs who more." It should sound like the ego arguing with itself.
- Slow right down on the last line. "My chat's still open" is said gently, not as a challenge.

## MUSIC + SFX (from the Epidemic Sound catalog)
- **Main track:** "Demure" by Ever So Blue. Solo piano; sad, sentimental, no vocals.
  - Alternates: "Goodnight Story" by Magnus Ludvigsson (sad, sentimental piano), or "The Letter" by Rikard From (softer and more hopeful, suits the ending).
- **SFX:**

- **Mix tip:** Keep the music low (about -20 dB under the voice). Cut it completely for 1 second on "Not one word."

### Production notes
- **Privacy:** Blur every real name and profile photo in the status-viewer screen recordings. A test or second number works well for staging the "Viewed by" list.
- **Shooting it alone:** Everything is a phone close-up, hands, or an empty room. No faces needed.
- **The loop matters:** The video ends just before Send is tapped and restarts on "Zero texts." That small question (*did they send it?*) is what makes people rewatch.
